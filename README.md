### About Zelene

**Zelene** is a community-driven digital ecosystem in development for **creators and communities**. Our goal is to bring content, memberships, subscriptions, tips, and digital experiences into a more connected environment, helping creators build direct relationships with their audiences.

We use **Solana** as a blockchain foundation for verifiable digital payments and services, with an emphasis on real-world utility rather than speculation.

### Our vision

A digital ecosystem where people can **create freely, connect meaningfully, and earn from their work**—supported by accessible technology, transparency, privacy, and greater control over their digital experiences.

### The Zelene ecosystem

| Initiative | Direction |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Zelene Platform** | Creator-focused tools for content, communities, memberships, payments, and digital experiences. |
| **Zelene Network** | Blockchain integrations, interoperability, on-chain tools, and infrastructure. |
| **Zelene Labs** | Research, experimentation, and future digital products and applications. |

*These initiatives describe the ecosystem's development direction; features and availability will evolve according to the roadmap.*

### Built with purpose

- **Real utility:** Technology should solve real problems.
- **Creator control:** More direct connections between creators and their communities.
- **Transparency:** Clear information and verifiable blockchain interactions.
- **Privacy and security:** Principles considered throughout product development.
- **Accessible experiences:** Practical tools for everyday digital activity.

### ZEL on Solana

**ZEL** is the ecosystem's **SPL utility token** on Solana, intended for specific products, services, and features within Zelene. Holding ZEL does not provide equity, profit-sharing rights, or ownership in the ecosystem.
